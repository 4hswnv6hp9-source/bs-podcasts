Qanday ma’lumot olinadi

Google hisobi bilan kirganingizda sayt faqat email manzilingiz, ismingiz va profil rasmingizni oladi.

Nima uchun ishlatiladi

Ma’lumot faqat sizni tanish, qiziqishlaringiz bo‘yicha podkast tavsiya qilish, sevimlilar va kunlik tinglash hisobini saqlash uchun kerak.

Qayerda saqlanadi

Tanlovlaringiz (qiziqishlar, sevimlilar, tinglash hisobi) sizning qurilmangizdagi brauzer xotirasida saqlanadi. Sayt egasi kirishlar sonini hisoblash uchun email va ismni yopiq jadvalda saqlashi mumkin. Ma’lumotlar sotilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

Ma’lumotni o‘chirish

Saytdagi “Chiqish” tugmasi qurilmangizdagi kirish ma’lumotini o‘chiradi. Google hisobingiz ruxsatlarini myaccount.google.com orqali bekor qilishingiz mumkin.

Privacy Policy

b’s podcasts is a free site for listening to English and Uzbek podcasts. When you sign in with Google, the site receives only your email address, name and profile picture. They are used to identify you, recommend podcasts and keep your favourites and daily listening count. Your preferences are stored in your browser on your device. The site owner may keep your email and name in a private sheet to count sign-ins. Data is never sold or shared with third parties. “Chiqish” (Sign out) removes the sign-in data from your device, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com.
